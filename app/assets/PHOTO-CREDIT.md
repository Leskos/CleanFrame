# Photograph credits

The fifteen launcher photographs and their derived material images use the Unsplash License. See [the source and credit register](../../docs/asset-sources.md) for each photographer and photo page, and [SOURCES.json](wallpapers/SOURCES.json) for runtime paths.

This fork adds four Brighton photographs from Wikimedia Commons:

- West Pier sunset by mattbuck, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brighton_MMB_31_West_Pier.jpg
- West Pier blue by Arild Vågen, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:West_Pier_April_2018_09.jpg
- West Pier sunrise by Christerajet, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sunrise_at_West_Pier.jpg
- Palace Pier gold by August Schwerdfeger, CC BY 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brighton_Palace_Pier_at_the_golden_hour_2019-05-28_-_2.jpg

Their bundled backgrounds, thumbnails and material images are cropped, resized, blurred and tinted adaptations; those of the CC BY-SA photos are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

These images are launcher backgrounds. Samsung Art Mode uses its own artwork and controls.
